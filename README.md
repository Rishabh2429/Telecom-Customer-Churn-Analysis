# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis using SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyzes telecom customer churn data using SQL to identify churn patterns, customer behavior, revenue impact, and retention opportunities. The analysis helps businesses understand why customers leave and supports data-driven retention strategies.

## Objectives

* Calculate overall customer churn rate.
* Identify key factors driving customer churn.
* Analyze revenue loss due to churn.
* Segment customers based on contract type, tenure, age, referrals, and services used.
* Provide actionable insights to improve customer retention.

## Dataset

The dataset contains customer demographics, subscription details, service usage, billing information, revenue metrics, and churn status for over 7,000 telecom customers.

## SQL Concepts Used

* Aggregations (COUNT, SUM, AVG)
* CASE Statements
* GROUP BY & ORDER BY
* Subqueries
* Conditional Aggregation
* KPI Calculations

## Key Business Questions Answered

1. Total customers and churned customers
2. Overall churn rate
3. Contract type with highest churn
4. Customer churn by tenure
5. Revenue lost due to churn
6. High-value customers at risk
7. Impact of internet type on churn
8. Churn by payment method
9. Service adoption vs churn
10. Churn by age group
11. Cities with highest churn
12. Top churn reasons
13. Churn category analysis
14. Referral impact on churn
15. Customers likely to churn
16. Customers suitable for retention campaigns

## Key Insights

* Month-to-month contracts showed the highest churn rate.
* Early-tenure customers were more likely to churn.
* Fiber internet users experienced higher churn.
* Customers using additional services showed better retention.
* Referral customers were more loyal than non-referred customers.
* Revenue loss from churn highlighted the financial impact of customer attrition.

## Business Impact

The analysis helps telecom companies identify churn risks, improve customer retention strategies, reduce revenue loss, and enhance customer lifetime value.
